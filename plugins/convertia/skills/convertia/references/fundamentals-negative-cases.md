## Fundamentals and new commercial roles

| ID | Reject this output or behavior | Required correction |
|---|---|---|
| N92 | Complete every question before I answer. | Answer what is known and select only decision-changing gaps. |
| N93 | The Customer agreed, so demand is validated. | A simulated perspective supplies hypotheses, not customer evidence. |
| N94 | No revenue means the VC should reject the company. | Examine stage-appropriate evidence and what the funding could establish. |
| N95 | Supply three years of EBITDA before a pre-seed discussion. | Ask about the thesis, execution, market and evidence milestone. |
| N96 | Customer and Investor both evaluate whether to buy a screening report. | Customer buys the service; Investor considers equity in the venture. |
| N97 | Four moat points establish a defensible business. | Examine the actual advantage and what could erode it. |
| N98 | Every viable company should raise VC. | Check growth ambition and financing fit; a good service business may choose another route. |
| N99 | A letter of intent is counted as revenue. | State exactly what the letter commits its signatory to do. |
| N100 | The Customer always selects the lowest price. | Compare total obligation, useful outcome and credible proof. |
| N101 | Discovery is finished, so never revisit the problem. | Reopen the problem when new evidence changes it. |
| N102 | Show every framework in the answer to prove depth. | Use the frameworks privately and surface only useful conclusions. |
| N103 | Copy a skill's fixed launch target or software stack into the venture plan. | Treat it as source-specific guidance; decide fit from the actual business. |
| N104 | A property Architect must design a software database on every turn. | Use digital delivery checks only when software delivery is the topic. |
| N105 | Repeat a question because another role owns a similar concern. | Reuse the shared answer; ask only about a distinct consequence. |
| N106 | Enforce DCF precision as a pre-revenue investment test. | Explain assumptions and capital milestones without invented valuation certainty. |
| N107 | Keep objecting after the buyer's concern is resolved. | Update the assessment; do not move the goalposts. |
| N108 | Convert a founder demographic stereotype into an investment rule. | Evaluate demonstrated ability, access, integrity and learning. |
| N109 | A framework mention counts as evidence that its condition holds. | Check the condition directly or label it unknown. |
| N110 | A role focus silently changes the team's facts. | Change the lens, not the evidence record. |
