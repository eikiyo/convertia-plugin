# Operations: atomic fundamentals

Use selectively after the shared bank. Each item tests one issue. These are diagnostic questions, not universal prerequisites. Reuse what is already known; surface only gaps that matter to the current decision. Technical and legal items are checks to investigate, not assertions that a rule applies.

## Activity and handoff

- OPS-001: What sequence delivers the promised result?
- OPS-002: Who owns each activity?
- OPS-003: What input starts each activity?
- OPS-004: What output completes each activity?
- OPS-005: Who accepts that output?
- OPS-006: What must the next person receive at handoff?
- OPS-007: Which handoff crosses an organisational boundary?
- OPS-008: What information is lost at that boundary?
- OPS-009: How does the customer learn the current status?
- OPS-010: What does the customer have to do before work can begin?

## Resources and capacity

- OPS-011: Which activity limits throughput?
- OPS-012: What capacity is available today?
- OPS-013: Which capacity figure is only assumed?
- OPS-014: Which competence is missing?
- OPS-015: Can that competence be purchased before hiring?
- OPS-016: Does the responsible person have enough time?
- OPS-017: What capacity expansion triggers a step cost?
- OPS-018: Which existing resource is sufficient for the pilot?
- OPS-019: Which resource must meet a professional standard from day one?
- OPS-020: What becomes unreliable at twice the workload?

## Partners and governance

- OPS-021: Who controls each essential asset?
- OPS-022: Which partner can stop delivery?
- OPS-023: What alternative exists if that partner withdraws?
- OPS-024: What does the partner gain from cooperation?
- OPS-025: What partner incentive conflicts with the service promise?
- OPS-026: What supplier quality evidence is required?
- OPS-027: Who bears the cost of supplier failure?
- OPS-028: What work should remain internal?
- OPS-029: What work can be reliably outsourced?
- OPS-030: What coordination cost does outsourcing add?

## Quality and recovery

- OPS-031: What is the acceptance standard for a screening?
- OPS-032: Who checks a material conclusion before delivery?
- OPS-033: What task creates the most rework?
- OPS-034: How does a failed check become visible?
- OPS-035: Who decides whether to correct, redo or stop the work?
- OPS-036: What recovery plan covers unavailable inputs?
- OPS-037: How is a customer notified of a discovered error?
- OPS-038: What must be retained to reconstruct the decision?
- OPS-039: What learning changed the process after the last failure?
- OPS-040: What service level can the team actually meet?

## Information and teams

- OPS-041: What information must move between activities?
- OPS-042: Who is responsible for its accuracy?
- OPS-043: What version is the current working record?
- OPS-044: Who can access the record?
- OPS-045: What knowledge exists only in one person's head?
- OPS-046: What happens when that person is unavailable?
- OPS-047: Who decides when specialists disagree?
- OPS-048: Can someone report an error without punishment?
- OPS-049: Do incentives reward the intended outcome?
- OPS-050: What customer commitment creates a delivery obligation?

## Adaptation and replication

- OPS-051: What should remain manual until the process is understood?
- OPS-052: What task is repeated consistently enough to automate?
- OPS-053: Is the uncertainty normal variation or an unknown change to the plan?
- OPS-054: What event triggers a plan review?
- OPS-055: Who may change the plan?
- OPS-056: What process can transfer unchanged to a new market?
- OPS-057: What process needs local adaptation?
- OPS-058: What operating evidence supports replication?
- OPS-059: Who verifies recovered materials before they are used?
- OPS-060: Is there a viable destination for the recovered output?
