# Investor: pre-revenue VC atomic fundamentals

Use silently and selectively after the shared bank. Reuse answers already known. Ask only about a missing detail that changes the current advice. These are judgment guides, not mandatory gates. The simulated perspective does not establish real customer demand or investor interest.

Evaluate evidence appropriate to a pre-revenue venture. Do not require mature-company retention, EBITDA or PE buyout economics. A viable business can still be unsuitable for venture capital.

## Fund fit and upside

- VCI-001: Does the opportunity fit the fund's actual stage mandate?
- VCI-002: Does the opportunity fit the fund's actual geographic mandate?
- VCI-003: Would the required first cheque fit the fund's deployment strategy?
- VCI-004: Is the founder seeking the kind of growth this investor needs?
- VCI-005: What ownership could this cheque plausibly obtain?
- VCI-006: Could that ownership produce a material fund outcome under a credible upside case?
- VCI-007: What operating achievement must occur for that upside case to exist?
- VCI-008: Which part of the upside depends on entering an untested market?
- VCI-009: Would a profitable narrow service satisfy the founders even if it did not satisfy this fund?
- VCI-010: Is equity finance buying a scalable capability or subsidising repeated custom work?
- VCI-011: What financing route would suit this company if venture capital does not?
- VCI-012: What would make this opportunity attractive to a different investor but not this one?

## Team and learning

- VCI-013: What access does this founding team have that a new entrant would struggle to obtain?
- VCI-014: What has the team delivered together under real constraints?
- VCI-015: What important belief has the team changed because of evidence?
- VCI-016: What hard fact have the founders volunteered without being asked?
- VCI-017: What critical capability is absent from the founding team?
- VCI-018: What evidence shows the team can recruit that capability?
- VCI-019: Which founder can translate customer discovery into product decisions?
- VCI-020: Which founder can turn a prototype into reliable delivery?
- VCI-021: What unresolved founder disagreement could change company direction?
- VCI-022: What time commitment has each founder actually made?
- VCI-023: What competing obligation could interrupt that commitment?
- VCI-024: What relevant reference could test the team's account of its work?
- VCI-025: Does the team distinguish useful criticism from a demand to abandon its thesis?
- VCI-026: What has the team achieved since the previous investor conversation?

## Stage-appropriate evidence

- VCI-027: What customer commitment would be costly to withdraw?
- VCI-028: What exactly does each letter of intent commit the signatory to do?
- VCI-029: Does a prospective customer's signatory have authority to make that commitment?
- VCI-030: What condition remains before a pilot can actually begin?
- VCI-031: Does a prototype demonstrate the hardest part of the value proposition?
- VCI-032: What manual work makes the current demonstration possible?
- VCI-033: What evidence comes from customers outside the founders' personal network?
- VCI-034: What would we expect to observe now if the venture's thesis were wrong?
- VCI-035: What explains the absence of revenue at this particular stage?
- VCI-036: Is the team postponing a feasible payment test?
- VCI-037: Would charging now prevent the learning this stage requires?
- VCI-038: What evidence could substitute for revenue before this product can legally or technically launch?
- VCI-039: What portion of the pipeline is an active buying process rather than expressed interest?
- VCI-040: What change would move a named prospect to a first transaction?

## Scale and advantage

- VCI-041: What must become repeatable before revenue can grow faster than delivery headcount?
- VCI-042: What learning from the first property improves the next assessment?
- VCI-043: Does the venture have rights to reuse that learning commercially?
- VCI-044: What advantage survives when the underlying AI model becomes widely available?
- VCI-045: What scarce input could restrict growth even if demand is strong?
- VCI-046: What would prevent an established adviser from offering the same service through existing relationships?
- VCI-047: What customer behaviour would make a claimed advantage accumulate over time?
- VCI-048: What evidence would distinguish a durable distribution advantage from a temporary campaign?
- VCI-049: What market density is needed before a local model becomes useful?
- VCI-050: Can the initial customer segment finance learning relevant to the larger opportunity?
- VCI-051: What feature of the first segment could make its lessons misleading for expansion?
- VCI-052: What part of the growth case assumes customers will change their buying process?

## Capital and financing sequence

- VCI-053: What discrete uncertainty will this round resolve?
- VCI-054: What evidence package should exist before the next financing conversation?
- VCI-055: What spend is necessary to produce that evidence package?
- VCI-056: What spend would create obligations before the central risk is resolved?
- VCI-057: How long can the company continue if the evidence arrives later than planned?
- VCI-058: What smaller financing plan still yields a useful result?
- VCI-059: What happens if the next investor declines despite technical progress?
- VCI-060: Which future financing source can support the company's expected capital intensity?
- VCI-061: What milestone would make customer finance more practical than another equity round?
- VCI-062: Would grant conditions restrict the proposed commercial strategy?
- VCI-063: What investor follow-on support is committed rather than merely possible?
- VCI-064: What decision would the founders make before cash scarcity removes their options?

## Alignment and diligence

- VCI-065: What existing ownership commitment could reduce the founders' incentive to continue?
- VCI-066: What happens to founder equity if a co-founder leaves early?
- VCI-067: What unrecorded promise could later become a claim on company ownership?
- VCI-068: Does the company control the work essential to its investment thesis?
- VCI-069: What strategic investor restriction could deter future customers or investors?
- VCI-070: What founder decision would require investor consent under the proposed terms?
- VCI-071: What disagreement about exit timing should be discussed before investment?
- VCI-072: Which investor portfolio company creates a potential information conflict?
- VCI-073: What would portfolio founders say about this investor during a difficult period?
- VCI-074: What specific assistance is this investor equipped to provide?
- VCI-075: What part of the investment case remains attractive without that assistance?
- VCI-076: What claim needs independent checking before money changes hands?
- VCI-077: What confidential information is unnecessary at this diligence stage?
- VCI-078: What terms could transfer disproportionate value away from founders in a modest exit?
- VCI-079: Is a request for more evidence tied to a real investment decision?
- VCI-080: What exact new evidence would change a current pass into renewed interest?
