# Business-model and commitment questions

This is a retained cross-role bank, not an eighth personality. Its legacy INV IDs preserve earlier references; they do not designate the new Investor. Customer owns buying questions, Finance owns owner distributions and valuation, Investor owns venture financing, and Roundtable routes strategy questions. Read only the relevant group. The dedicated Investor bank uses VCI IDs.

Use selectively after the shared bank. Each item tests one issue. These are diagnostic questions, not universal prerequisites. Reuse what is already known; surface only gaps that matter to the current decision. Technical and legal items are checks to investigate, not assertions that a rule applies.

Buying questions INV-001 through INV-020 now live in customer.md, with the same IDs. Do not maintain a second copy here.

## Business-model coherence

- INV-021: Does each essential stakeholder have a reason to participate?
- INV-022: Which activities create additional value together?
- INV-023: Which combined activities only add complexity?
- INV-024: Which changed building block invalidates another assumption?
- INV-025: Is the business coherent without rapid growth?
- INV-026: What strategic choice goes beyond describing the business model?
- INV-027: Which customers will the venture deliberately not serve?
- INV-028: What claim about value creation lacks a plausible mechanism?

## Market and channels

- INV-029: How large is the reachable initial segment?
- INV-030: What evidence supports that reachable market estimate?
- INV-031: Can the team reach buyers through a credible channel?
- INV-032: Does the channel remain economical beyond founder-led sales?
- INV-033: Who controls access to the buyer?
- INV-034: Could the customer bypass Convertia after meeting the supplier?
- INV-035: What adoption barrier remains after the economics work?
- INV-036: What local mismatch could defeat expansion?
- INV-037: Which model components can transfer to the next market?
- INV-038: What must be learned before a larger market commitment?

## Defensibility and growth

- INV-039: What could a competitor copy immediately?
- INV-040: What advantage remains after copying?
- INV-041: What substitute could remove the need for the service?
- INV-042: Who has the bargaining power to take its margin?
- INV-043: What internal inefficiency could erode the advantage?
- INV-044: Does an additional participant improve value for an existing participant?
- INV-045: What evidence supports the claimed network effect?
- INV-046: At what geographic scope does that effect operate?
- INV-047: Can customers use several competing services?
- INV-048: What makes customers stay voluntarily?
- INV-049: Which growth mechanism reinforces future growth?
- INV-050: Where could that reinforcing mechanism stop?

## Commitment and accountability

- INV-051: Are we funding an experiment or an established operation?

- INV-053: Which readiness weakness could invalidate stronger dimensions?
