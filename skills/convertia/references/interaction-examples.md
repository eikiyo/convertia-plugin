# Text interaction examples

## Broad question
User: Dissect our business model.

**Test a tightly scoped screening offer before committing to more software.**

**Buyer**
- Choose one type of property owner with a live building decision.
- Speak to the person who can approve the spending.
- Record what the buyer actually commits. Interest, project access and payment are different signals.

**Offer**
- Screen one building and one proposed use.
- Define the required files, checks and exclusions before quoting.
- Sell a decision about further study, not an approval to convert.

**Economics**
- Count delivery time, expert review and direct costs.
- Track sales work and overhead separately.
- Price extra work before doing it.

**Implications**
Conditional chain for accepting incomplete files:
1. Missing inputs create extra follow-up.
2. If that work is unpaid, less money remains from the fee.
3. If it repeats, the service can become uneconomic.
Stop at order 3: delivery records are needed to support further effects.
Intervene before quoting: check file completeness.

**Next actions**
1. Interview one buyer group about recent building decisions.
2. Define the first screening's inputs and deliverables.
3. Quote a real case and record the purchasing decision.

***Tip: Ask for an Operations focus to define the first screening checklist.***

This is a strategy example, not a sourced market claim. Do not append this explanatory sentence to a user report.

## Narrow follow-up
User: What do you mean by bundle?

**A bundle is several services sold together for one price.**

- **Example:** initial building checks, a cost estimate and a recommendation in one screening package.
- **Risk:** extra work can consume the fee unless the package has clear limits.

## Updated assumption
User: What if delivery costs rise from SEK 12,000 to SEK 15,000 and our fee stays SEK 20,000?

**The amount left after delivery costs falls from SEK 8,000 to SEK 5,000.**

- Old: SEK 20,000 - SEK 12,000 = SEK 8,000.
- New: SEK 20,000 - SEK 15,000 = SEK 5,000.
- That still has to cover sales work, overhead and profit. All figures must use the same VAT basis.
