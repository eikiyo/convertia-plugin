# Shared fundamentals

Use silently and selectively. Reuse existing answers. These questions guide judgment; they are not completion requirements.

## Decision and scope

- FND-001: What decision is the user trying to make now?
- FND-002: What would change if that decision went the other way?
- FND-003: Which stage is this venture actually in?
- FND-004: What is explicitly outside this discussion?
- FND-005: Which constraint most limits the next step?
- FND-006: What has changed since the last assessment?

## People and demand

- FND-007: Who is the core customer segment?
- FND-008: What makes someone part of that segment?
- FND-009: Who uses the service?
- FND-010: Who pays for it?
- FND-011: Who benefits from its outcome?
- FND-012: Who approves the purchase?
- FND-013: Who can block the purchase?
- FND-014: Whose incentives conflict with the buyer's?

## Problem and alternative

- FND-015: What job is the customer trying to complete?
- FND-016: What event makes that job urgent?
- FND-017: How does the customer handle it today?
- FND-018: What does the current approach cost the customer?
- FND-019: How often does the problem occur?
- FND-020: What happens if the customer does nothing?
- FND-021: Which observed behaviour supports the stated problem?

## Offer and business logic

- FND-022: What concrete outcome does the offer promise?
- FND-023: What is the smallest useful deliverable?
- FND-024: What is excluded from the offer?
- FND-025: Why would the customer choose this over the current alternative?
- FND-026: Who performs each essential activity?
- FND-027: What must a partner provide?
- FND-028: What exactly generates revenue?
- FND-029: What must be spent to deliver one unit?

## Evidence and uncertainty

- FND-030: Which claim is an observed fact?
- FND-031: Which claim is still an assumption?
- FND-032: What evidence would contradict the main assumption?
- FND-033: What decision depends on the weakest evidence?
- FND-034: What relevant evidence is already in this conversation?
- FND-035: What assumption has become stale?
- FND-036: Which uncertainty can be reduced cheaply?
- FND-037: Which uncertainty must be accepted for now?

## Progress and consequences

- FND-038: What is the cheapest useful next test?
- FND-039: What result would change the recommendation?
- FND-040: Who owns the next deliverable?
- FND-041: What dependency must be resolved first?
- FND-042: Who could be harmed by the proposed action?
- FND-043: What is reversible about this decision?
- FND-044: What is the earliest point to stop an avoidable loss?

## Actor distinctions and relationships

- FND-045: Who consumes the resulting product or outcome?
- FND-046: Is the consumer different from the person using the service?
- FND-047: What ongoing relationship does this customer expect?
- FND-048: Which channel brings the customer to the offer?
- FND-049: Which channel delivers the promised value?
- FND-050: Does a different segment need a different business model?
