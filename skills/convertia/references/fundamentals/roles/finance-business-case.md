# Finance & Business Case: atomic fundamentals

Use selectively after the shared bank. Each item tests one issue. These are diagnostic questions, not universal prerequisites. Reuse what is already known; surface only gaps that matter to the current decision. Technical and legal items are checks to investigate, not assertions that a rule applies.

## Revenue and price

- FIN-001: What is the unit of sale?
- FIN-002: What triggers an invoice?
- FIN-003: Which customer pays for each revenue stream?
- FIN-004: What price has been tested in an actual purchase?
- FIN-005: What alternative anchors the buyer's price comparison?
- FIN-006: What customer benefit justifies the price?
- FIN-007: What non-price cost does adoption impose?
- FIN-008: Does the pricing unit match the buyer's decision?
- FIN-009: Which discount changes the economics materially?
- FIN-010: Which tax treatment is assumed in the quote?

## Cost and scope

- FIN-011: What does one completed unit cost to deliver?
- FIN-012: Is founder time included in that cost?
- FIN-013: Is external review included in that cost?
- FIN-014: Is expected rework included in that cost?
- FIN-015: Is post-delivery support included in that cost?
- FIN-016: Which expense occurs before the first sale?
- FIN-017: Which expense grows with each customer?
- FIN-018: Which expense increases in capacity steps?
- FIN-019: Which bundle component loses money?
- FIN-020: Who pays for work outside the agreed scope?

## Viability

- FIN-021: What remains from the fee after direct delivery costs?
- FIN-022: What fixed costs must that amount cover?
- FIN-023: What sales volume covers those fixed costs?
- FIN-024: Can available capacity deliver that volume?
- FIN-025: What utilisation does the calculation assume?
- FIN-026: What evidence supports the utilisation assumption?
- FIN-027: What costs are excluded from the stated margin?
- FIN-028: Are transaction volume and revenue being confused?
- FIN-029: Are booked revenue and collected cash being confused?
- FIN-030: Does growth improve unit economics or extend a subsidy?

## Sales and repeat demand

- FIN-031: What does acquiring a paying customer cost?
- FIN-032: Does that cost include founder sales time?
- FIN-033: How long until acquisition spending is recovered?
- FIN-034: What evidence supports repeat purchases?
- FIN-035: What observed retention supports the lifetime-value estimate?
- FIN-036: What price sensitivity has actually been tested?
- FIN-037: What evidence shows free users become paying customers?
- FIN-038: Is a free pilot counted as research or revenue?

## Cash and capital

- FIN-039: When is customer cash collected?
- FIN-040: When must suppliers be paid?
- FIN-041: What working-capital gap exists between those dates?
- FIN-042: How much cash does growth consume before payment?
- FIN-043: What cash commitment is unavoidable?
- FIN-044: What expenditure can be staged?
- FIN-045: What funding is already secured?
- FIN-046: What funding is merely expected?
- FIN-047: What milestone must the next funding buy?
- FIN-048: Does the funding instrument fit the activity's risk?
- FIN-049: What can the team afford to lose on this experiment?
- FIN-050: What happens to runway if funding is delayed?

## Scenarios and owner economics

- FIN-051: Which assumption most changes the recommendation?
- FIN-052: What happens if the buyer pays late?
- FIN-053: What happens if fewer prospects buy?
- FIN-054: What happens if delivery needs more hours?
- FIN-055: What happens if the key subsidy disappears?
- FIN-056: What scenario combines plausible adverse changes?
- FIN-057: What economic benefit does the property owner gain against doing nothing?
- FIN-058: Are property returns being confused with Convertia's service profit?
- FIN-059: Are option comparisons using the same time horizon?
- FIN-060: Are option comparisons using the same area basis?
- FIN-061: Who finances ongoing maintenance?
- FIN-062: What cost estimate has a supplier independently checked?


## Additional fundamentals from the corpus

Use asset valuation and discounted-cash-flow questions only when the decision and available inputs make them relevant. They are not requirements for a pre-revenue VC investment case.

- FIN-063: What capital investment must precede additional revenue?
- FIN-064: What replacement investment is absent from the forecast?
- FIN-065: What terminal or residual value is assumed?
- FIN-066: What discount-rate assumption drives the comparison?
- FIN-067: What asset utilisation makes an ownership model preferable to rental?
- FIN-068: Who receives the financial benefit when a different actor pays the cost?

## Further decision checks

- FIN-069: Which decision must this financial model support?
- FIN-070: Which source controls each assumption used across the model?
- FIN-071: Which scenario is currently active?
- FIN-072: Do the cash flows and discount rate refer to the same capital providers?
- FIN-073: Is the stated valuation enterprise value or equity value?
- FIN-074: What debt and cash adjustments connect enterprise value to equity value?
- FIN-075: What share of the estimated value comes from the terminal value?
- FIN-076: What evidence supports the assumed steady state?
- FIN-077: Does the terminal-value method fit the expected life of the business?
- FIN-078: Which service depends on a cross-subsidy?
- FIN-079: What happens to delivery if that subsidy ends?
- FIN-080: Are customer development payments being mistaken for repeat product revenue?
- FIN-081: Who finances end-of-life obligations?
