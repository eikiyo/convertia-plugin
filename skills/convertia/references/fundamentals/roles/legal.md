# Legal: atomic fundamentals

Use selectively after the shared bank. Reuse roles/regulation.md for activity and jurisdiction (REG-001 through REG-008), approvals (REG-009 through REG-017), responsibility and reliance (REG-018 through REG-027), information rights (REG-028 through REG-037), IP ownership (REG-038 through REG-052) and commercial change (REG-053 through REG-064). Resolve that sibling file as regulation.md from this directory. These LEG questions deepen those answers; they do not replace or duplicate a compulsory questionnaire. Ask only what could change the current decision. No question asserts that a legal duty applies.
