# Architect & Engineer: atomic fundamentals

Use selectively after the shared bank. Each item tests one issue. These are diagnostic questions, not universal prerequisites. Reuse what is already known; surface only gaps that matter to the current decision. Technical and legal items are checks to investigate, not assertions that a rule applies.

## Required function and alternatives

- ARC-001: What physical outcome must the solution deliver?
- ARC-002: What minimum performance makes that outcome useful?
- ARC-003: Which need was observed rather than assumed?
- ARC-004: Can the need be stated without prescribing a solution?
- ARC-005: What existing solution provides the baseline?
- ARC-006: Could keeping the asset unchanged meet the need?
- ARC-007: Could a smaller intervention provide enough value?
- ARC-008: What unwanted effect could the intervention create?

## Property and input quality

- ARC-009: Which property is being assessed?
- ARC-010: What is its current use?
- ARC-011: What proposed use is being assessed?
- ARC-012: Which drawing version represents the actual building?
- ARC-013: What measurement has not been verified?
- ARC-014: What condition cannot be established from the supplied plans?
- ARC-015: Which input conflicts with another input?
- ARC-016: What missing input most affects the conclusion?
- ARC-017: What input requires a site inspection?
- ARC-018: Who can verify the critical measurement?

## Physical feasibility

- ARC-019: What geometric constraint could prevent the proposed layout?
- ARC-020: What structural assumption needs confirmation?
- ARC-021: What fire-safety assumption needs specialist review?
- ARC-022: What access requirement affects the design?
- ARC-023: What daylight condition needs assessment?
- ARC-024: What ventilation capacity does the proposed use require?
- ARC-025: What services capacity is already available?
- ARC-026: What hazardous-material uncertainty affects the intervention?
- ARC-027: What acoustic condition matters to the proposed use?
- ARC-028: What change to the building envelope may be necessary?

## System and interface

- ARC-029: Which component could invalidate the whole solution?
- ARC-030: What must exist before that component works?
- ARC-031: What changes elsewhere if this component changes?
- ARC-032: Which interface is least reliable?
- ARC-033: Which input specification must a supplier meet?
- ARC-034: Who verifies that specification?
- ARC-035: Which part can be standardised?
- ARC-036: Which part requires local adaptation?
- ARC-037: What existing solution can be reused?
- ARC-038: Which design choice creates dependence on one supplier?

## Testing and reliability

- ARC-039: What does the prototype actually demonstrate?
- ARC-040: What does it leave untested?
- ARC-041: Was the test environment representative of use?
- ARC-042: What stressful condition was excluded?
- ARC-043: Have the components been tested together?
- ARC-044: What is the smallest safe test that could disprove feasibility?
- ARC-045: How would a false-positive screening harm the owner?
- ARC-046: How would a false-negative screening harm the owner?
- ARC-047: What failure would become visible only after handover?
- ARC-048: What evidence would justify repeating this solution elsewhere?

## Whole life and handover

- ARC-049: What maintenance does the proposed solution require?
- ARC-050: Who will perform that maintenance?
- ARC-051: Can a failed part be repaired independently?
- ARC-052: Can the solution adapt to a different future use?
- ARC-053: What prevents disassembly or reuse?
- ARC-054: What evidence supports the quality of recovered materials?
- ARC-055: Does the intervention shift harm to another life-cycle stage?
- ARC-056: What baseline makes the environmental comparison meaningful?
- ARC-057: What support makes the solution usable without its designer?
- ARC-058: What technical uncertainty must remain visible in the final recommendation?


## Additional fundamentals from the corpus

- ARC-059: Could solving this technical problem worsen another stakeholder's problem?
- ARC-060: What trade-off exists between minimum compliance and the user's desired performance?
- ARC-061: What baseline would a qualified reviewer use to compare interventions?
- ARC-062: What makes the proposed solution a complete usable service rather than a working component?
