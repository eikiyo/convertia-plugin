# Customer: atomic fundamentals

Use silently and selectively after the shared bank. Reuse answers already known. Ask only about a missing detail that changes the current advice. These are judgment guides, not mandatory gates. The simulated perspective does not establish real customer demand or investor interest.

The inherited INV-001 to INV-020 IDs below now belong to Customer and are preserved for continuity.

## Buying decision

- INV-001: What decision will the buyer make using this service?
- INV-002: What is the consequence of a wrong decision?
- INV-003: Which budget pays for this purchase?
- INV-004: What event releases that budget?
- INV-005: Who must endorse the purchase?
- INV-006: What makes the purchase worthwhile now?
- INV-007: What happens if the buyer waits?
- INV-008: What is the smallest offer the buyer can approve?
- INV-009: What proof does this particular buyer require?
- INV-010: What purchase friction could exceed the benefit?

## Choice and differentiation

- INV-011: Which alternative is the buyer most likely to choose?
- INV-012: Why is Convertia better for this specific buyer?
- INV-013: Is the difference important or merely novel?
- INV-014: What feature adds cost without changing the buyer's decision?
- INV-015: What industry convention could be eliminated?
- INV-016: What feature could be reduced without losing value?
- INV-017: What customer outcome should be improved?
- INV-018: What missing outcome could be created?
- INV-019: Is the offer complete enough for a cautious buyer?
- INV-020: Does early-adopter proof persuade the next segment?

## Specification and usefulness

- CUS-001: What file or service would I receive after paying?
- CUS-002: Can I inspect a representative deliverable before committing?
- CUS-003: What decision could I make from that deliverable without another consultant?
- CUS-004: Which promised conclusion would still require a separate paid assessment?
- CUS-005: What level of uncertainty would make the result unusable for me?
- CUS-006: Can my existing adviser use the deliverable without rebuilding it?
- CUS-007: What information must I collect specifically for your service?
- CUS-008: What happens to the quote when my records are incomplete?
- CUS-009: Which part of the result is specific to my property?
- CUS-010: How will I distinguish an unavailable answer from a negative finding?
- CUS-011: Can I buy the useful component without the rest of the bundle?
- CUS-012: What would I lose by buying only that component?

## Payment and commercial terms

- CUS-013: What total amount would leave my budget under the quoted scope?
- CUS-014: What event would permit the supplier to increase that amount?
- CUS-015: Can additional work begin without my written agreement?
- CUS-016: What am I paying for if the screening finds no viable conversion?
- CUS-017: Is payment tied to effort, delivery or an accepted result?
- CUS-018: What deposit would be at risk before I receive useful work?
- CUS-019: What cancellation charge would apply before delivery starts?
- CUS-020: What cancellation charge would apply after delivery starts?
- CUS-021: What happens to payment while I dispute a material defect?
- CUS-022: What revision is included in the original fee?
- CUS-023: What fee applies if I supply corrected information?
- CUS-024: Does the advertised price depend on purchasing another service?
- CUS-025: Does a discounted pilot create a later purchase obligation?
- CUS-026: What future price applies after the pilot ends?
- CUS-027: What commitment would a subscription require when I have no active projects?
- CUS-028: Can unused purchased capacity expire?

## Internal buying process

- CUS-029: What document must I submit to request internal approval?
- CUS-030: Does this purchase need a competing quote under our actual policy?
- CUS-031: Can this supplier complete our vendor onboarding in time?
- CUS-032: Which internal department has not yet reviewed the offer?
- CUS-033: Who will defend the purchase when the sponsor is absent?
- CUS-034: What concern would make my procurement team reject this supplier?
- CUS-035: Which proposed term would require an exception to our standard contract?
- CUS-036: Does the purchase fit the current budget period?
- CUS-037: Can a pilot be approved through a different process from full deployment?
- CUS-038: What outcome must I report to justify spending again?
- CUS-039: Would the supplier's referral fee undermine my internal business case?
- CUS-040: What would make my finance team hold the invoice?

## Adoption and switching

- CUS-041: What existing task disappears when I adopt this service?
- CUS-042: What existing task would I still have to perform?
- CUS-043: Must I change another supplier to use this offer?
- CUS-044: Can I test the service while retaining my current workflow?
- CUS-045: What training would my staff need before receiving value?
- CUS-046: Who will do the extra coordination work inside my organisation?
- CUS-047: Can I use the result in the software my team already uses?
- CUS-048: What would make reverting to my existing approach difficult?
- CUS-049: Can I take my project records away if I stop using the service?
- CUS-050: What would happen to my unfinished project if the supplier closes?
- CUS-051: What deadline must delivery meet to influence my actual decision?
- CUS-052: What useful partial result is available if that deadline is missed?

## Trust and repeat purchase

- CUS-053: What prior work resembles the risk of my project?
- CUS-054: Can I speak to a relevant customer with their permission?
- CUS-055: Who will answer a detailed challenge to the report's assumptions?
- CUS-056: How can I inspect the basis of a conclusion without trusting a score?
- CUS-057: How will I know when the assessment is too old to rely on?
- CUS-058: What change to my property would require a new assessment?
- CUS-059: What information will be shared with prospective partners?
- CUS-060: Can I refuse publicity without changing the service terms?
- CUS-061: What happens if I do not want my project used to improve the service?
- CUS-062: Who will respond when I challenge an error after delivery?
- CUS-063: What would make the second purchase easier than the first?
- CUS-064: What genuine recurring need would justify paying repeatedly?
- CUS-065: Would I recommend the service if no incentive were offered?
- CUS-066: What disappointment would stop me referring another owner?
- CUS-067: Is the offer still attractive without the launch discount?
- CUS-068: Would I exchange access to my project for this benefit before paying?
- CUS-069: What competing priority could absorb the money reserved for this purchase?
- CUS-070: What single unresolved detail currently prevents my commitment?
