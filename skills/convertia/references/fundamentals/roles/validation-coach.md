# Validation Coach: atomic fundamentals

Use selectively after the shared bank. Each item tests one issue. These are diagnostic questions, not universal prerequisites. Reuse what is already known; surface only gaps that matter to the current decision. Technical and legal items are checks to investigate, not assertions that a rule applies.

## Discovery

- VAL-001: What happened the last time the customer faced this problem?
- VAL-002: What did the customer actually do?
- VAL-003: What did the customer spend?
- VAL-004: What alternative did the customer try?
- VAL-005: What solution did the customer abandon?
- VAL-006: What behaviour contradicts the stated preference?
- VAL-007: What observation could corroborate the interview?
- VAL-008: What important stakeholder is missing from discovery?
- VAL-009: Whose experience differs from the founder's account?
- VAL-010: What evidence suggests the problem is worth solving?

## Definition

- VAL-011: What precise belief is being tested?
- VAL-012: What mechanism explains that belief?
- VAL-013: What decision depends on the result?
- VAL-014: What observation would contradict the belief?
- VAL-015: Is the assumed cause different from the visible symptom?
- VAL-016: What competing explanation fits the evidence?
- VAL-017: Which segment is the current hypothesis about?
- VAL-018: Is the proposed problem statement already prescribing a solution?
- VAL-019: What would solving the problem look like to that segment?
- VAL-020: What important fact remains unknown after problem definition?

## Test design

- VAL-021: Is the test about demand, feasibility, price or delivery?
- VAL-022: What is the most consequential untested assumption?
- VAL-023: How late could failure of that assumption be discovered?
- VAL-024: What is the cheapest credible test of that assumption?
- VAL-025: What result would support the hypothesis?
- VAL-026: What rejection threshold was set before this test began?
- VAL-027: What result would remain inconclusive?
- VAL-028: What decision follows from each result?
- VAL-029: What is the maximum affordable cost of the test?
- VAL-030: What is the time limit for the test?
- VAL-031: What alternative solution deserves comparison?
- VAL-032: What makes the test safe enough for participants?

## Sample and interview quality

- VAL-033: Are participants from the intended segment?
- VAL-034: Are buyers missing from a sample of users?
- VAL-035: Have non-buyers been included?
- VAL-036: Have former users been included?
- VAL-037: Is the interview question leading?
- VAL-038: Is the interview turning into a sales pitch?
- VAL-039: Does the answer describe past behaviour or imagined behaviour?
- VAL-040: Is politeness being counted as demand?
- VAL-041: What actual time or money has the participant committed?
- VAL-042: Does the sample support the strength of the claim?

## Measurement and causality

- VAL-043: What exactly does the chosen metric measure?
- VAL-044: Does that metric measure the outcome that matters?
- VAL-045: What useful baseline exists?
- VAL-046: Can the same outcome be observed another way?
- VAL-047: Are several test variables changing at once?
- VAL-048: What part of the result comes from the test setup?
- VAL-049: Is one respondent reporting both cause and effect?
- VAL-050: What omitted factor could explain the result?
- VAL-051: Could the direction of causality be reversed?
- VAL-052: Is correlation being treated as causation?
- VAL-053: Can a respondent honestly say they do not know?
- VAL-054: Is contradictory evidence retained?

## Learning and next commitment

- VAL-055: What did the result change in the business model?
- VAL-056: What remains uncertain after the test?
- VAL-057: Should the experiment be repeated, revised or stopped?
- VAL-058: What would justify narrowing the segment?
- VAL-059: Which model component changes in the proposed pivot?
- VAL-060: What remains useful if the hypothesis fails?
- VAL-061: Is the team repeating a test whose answer is already known?
- VAL-062: Is the team testing easy details to avoid its central claim?
- VAL-063: Could incremental feedback reject an unfamiliar but valuable idea too early?
- VAL-064: What larger test is needed before scaling?
- VAL-065: Is technical success being mistaken for demand?
- VAL-066: Is demand being mistaken for profitable delivery?
- VAL-067: What evidence justifies transferring this finding to another market?
- VAL-068: What new event makes an old conclusion worth revisiting?


## Additional fundamentals from the corpus

- VAL-069: Do stakeholders agree on what the problem is?
- VAL-070: Whose problem definition are we treating as the default?
- VAL-071: What can an extreme user reveal that an average user may not?
- VAL-072: What outlier observation deserves investigation?
- VAL-073: What functional job is the customer trying to complete?
- VAL-074: What social outcome does the customer seek?
- VAL-075: What emotional concern shapes the customer's choice?
- VAL-076: What desired gain is not addressed by the current offer?
- VAL-077: What different solution approaches were considered before choosing this one?
- VAL-078: What output will the pilot produce?
- VAL-079: What outcome should that output cause?
- VAL-080: What evidence connects the output to that outcome?
- VAL-081: What wider impact could follow from that outcome?
- VAL-082: What evidence connects the outcome to that impact?
- VAL-083: Has a partner commitment changed the venture's objective?
- VAL-084: Whose resistance suggests the current interpretation is incomplete?

## Further decision checks

- VAL-085: Which strategic objective does this metric serve?
- VAL-086: Was this metric selected mainly because its data were easy to obtain?
- VAL-087: Who must act when this metric changes?
- VAL-088: Could improving this metric worsen the overall outcome?
- VAL-089: Which chosen metrics reward conflicting behaviours?
- VAL-090: What does collecting this measurement cost?
- VAL-091: Does the test audience tolerate defects that mainstream customers will reject?
- VAL-092: Is customer resistance postponement, rejection or active opposition?
- VAL-093: Is one lead customer's requested customisation distorting the target offer?
- VAL-094: Is this customer acting as a co-developer, adviser or reference customer?
- VAL-095: Does the idea draw on different kinds of knowledge or merely more contributors?
- VAL-096: What evidence separates an intention from an action already taken?
