# Innovation fundamentals

Use these questions selectively alongside the shared bank and canonical Double Diamond guidance. Reuse known answers. Ask only about missing fundamentals that affect the current decision. Owners name existing council roles; this bank does not create additional personalities. These are diagnostic adaptations, not a validated scoring instrument or compulsory stages. Do not import large-company requirements into a pre-revenue venture. Customer judges lived usefulness and payment; Investor tests venture potential under uncertainty.

## Double Diamond: opening the problem space

Owner: Validation Coach. Customer supplies the lived experience.

- INN-003: What happens before the moment described as the problem?
- INN-004: What happens after that moment?
- INN-006: What problem would remain if the proposed product disappeared?
- INN-007: Which apparently separate complaints share the same underlying constraint?
- INN-008: Which similar complaints have different causes?
- INN-010: Has the team closed exploration because it found evidence or because its preferred solution needed a problem?

## Double Diamond: choosing the problem

Owner: Validation Coach. Investor challenges the strategic choice.

- INN-011: What criterion made this problem more important than the alternatives?
- INN-012: Who chose that criterion?
- INN-014: What disagreement remains about the chosen problem?
- INN-015: What consequence follows from choosing the wrong problem now?
- INN-017: Does the selected problem fit resources the team can realistically mobilise?
- INN-019: Which stakeholder's priorities dominated the choice?
- INN-020: What important need was excluded by that choice?

## Double Diamond: opening and selecting solutions

Owner: Architect & Engineer. Validation Coach owns the comparison method.

- INN-023: Which candidate changes behaviour rather than adding technology?
- INN-024: Which candidate reduces the need for coordination?
- INN-025: Are candidate solutions different approaches or only variants of the favourite?
- INN-027: What is the cheapest representation that exposes each candidate's main weakness?
- INN-028: Has the team mistaken a polished prototype for a superior approach?
- INN-030: What design decision should remain reversible until comparison is complete?

## Double Diamond: delivery and return loops

Owner: Operations. Customer judges actual usefulness.

- INN-032: Which benefit depends on assistance that real customers will not receive?
- INN-035: Which delivery constraint invalidates the selected concept?
- INN-036: What must be learned from use after initial acceptance?
- INN-037: What evidence separates successful onboarding from a useful ongoing service?
- INN-038: Which user workaround should change the design?
- INN-039: Which workaround indicates the team solved the wrong problem?

## Matching action to uncertainty

Owner: Operations and Validation Coach.

- INN-041: Is the disputed relationship stable enough for expert analysis to resolve it?
- INN-042: Does the relationship change when people react to the intervention?
- INN-043: Which routine is being applied outside the conditions where it worked?
- INN-044: Which early warning could frontline staff see before management sees it?
- INN-045: Can disagreement reveal a mistaken classification of the situation?
- INN-046: What bounded experiment could reveal a useful pattern?
- INN-047: What damage could that experiment cause outside its immediate participants?
- INN-048: Who can stop the experiment before its effects spread?
- INN-049: Which emerging behaviour should be supported rather than standardised away?
- INN-050: What immediate instability must be contained before useful learning can resume?

## Ecosystem dependence and bargaining

Owner: Operations. Investor challenges durable advantage.

- INN-051: Which complementary service is required for the customer to obtain the promised result?
- INN-052: Which necessary contributor has no direct transaction with Convertia?
- INN-053: Is our dependence on a partner stronger than its dependence on us?
- INN-054: What changes if a specialised partner capability becomes widely available?
- INN-055: Which interface could be standardised to reduce coordination cost?
- INN-056: Would that standardisation remove our bargaining advantage?
- INN-057: What cost makes joint ownership preferable to contracting?
- INN-058: What coordination mechanism would make separate ownership workable?
- INN-059: Which actor can benefit from the shared offering without contributing to it?
- INN-060: What makes contributors willing to keep investing after the pilot?

## External viability and contested control

Owner: Regulation and Operations. Investor tests exposure.

- INN-061: Which established practice prevents adoption even if the partners agree?
- INN-062: What infrastructure outside the partnership must already exist?
- INN-063: Which accepted professional role would the new offering displace?
- INN-064: Who regards the proposed coordinator as legitimate?
- INN-065: What decision can the coordinator influence but not control?
- INN-066: Which partners compete for the same scarce resource?
- INN-067: Which partner's business model depends on a revenue stream another partner expects to own?
- INN-068: What evidence would distinguish a coordination failure from an unviable market setting?
- INN-069: Can the offering be changed to fit the environment without losing its customer value?
- INN-070: Which outside change would make the present partnership unnecessary?

## Open innovation and ability to use knowledge

Owner: Operations and Architect & Engineer.

- INN-071: Who inside the team can evaluate the external knowledge being acquired?
- INN-072: What must be translated before that knowledge can enter daily work?
- INN-073: Which valuable external insight is being rejected because of where it came from?
- INN-074: What internal knowledge is required to recognise a good external proposal?
- INN-075: Which educational perspective is absent from the problem-solving team?
- INN-076: Can team members combine their different knowledge rather than merely represent different backgrounds?
- INN-077: What time is reserved for understanding external contributions?
- INN-078: What acquired knowledge has reached an actual delivery decision?
- INN-079: Which contribution should be licensed or partnered instead of recreated?
- INN-080: What knowledge can be shared without surrendering the venture's essential advantage?

## Innovation measurement without distorted behaviour

Owner: Validation Coach and Operations.

- INN-081: Which partner outcome must be measured for our own metric to mean anything?
- INN-082: Does the metric reward selecting safe projects over exploring necessary uncertainty?
- INN-083: What failure would count as useful learning rather than poor execution?
- INN-085: Which metric should be retired because it no longer changes a decision?
- INN-086: What delay separates the measured activity from its intended outcome?
- INN-087: Does the measurement period penalise work whose benefits emerge later?
- INN-088: What important relationship disappears when results are averaged?
- INN-089: Does the person accountable for a metric control the action required to improve it?
- INN-090: What comparison would expose improvement caused only by easier case selection?

## Circular and sustainability innovation

Owner: Architect & Engineer, Operations and Investor.

- INN-091: Does the sustainability ambition require a different business model rather than a more efficient current process?
- INN-092: Which existing norm makes the proposed circular practice difficult to accept?
- INN-093: Does the revenue model benefit when the customer consumes more material?
- INN-094: Which current incentive discourages extending an asset's useful life?
- INN-095: What new partner arrangement is required to retain value after first use?
- INN-096: Which near-term target suppresses exploration of a more substantial change?
- INN-097: Is the environmental improvement absolute or only an improvement per unit?
- INN-098: Could greater uptake cancel the environmental saving per use?
- INN-099: Which social cost sits outside the environmental calculation?
- INN-100: Who is responsible for the claimed benefit after the product or asset changes hands?

## Portfolio choices and changing dependencies

Owner: Investor and Operations. These are strategic diagnostics, not mature-company valuation requirements.

- INN-101: Which experiment addresses a different uncertainty from the rest of the portfolio?
- INN-102: Are apparently different experiments exposed to the same hidden assumption?
- INN-103: Which small experiment could preserve an option the current plan would close?
- INN-104: Which failed result would still leave a reusable capability?
- INN-105: Which experiment consumes a scarce resource needed by the strongest alternative?
- INN-106: What evidence would justify moving resources from refinement to exploration?
- INN-107: Which existing success makes the team less willing to notice a changed context?
- INN-108: What capability must be retired for the new approach to work?
- INN-109: Which partner investment becomes stranded if the shared offering changes?
- INN-110: What change to the offering would reduce the number of actors needed to deliver it?
- INN-111: Which component's failure would make otherwise successful components worthless?
- INN-112: What must be coordinated tightly because it cannot yet be separated into independent parts?
- INN-113: Which dependency could become asymmetric as the venture grows?
- INN-114: Who benefits if interfaces remain difficult to change?
- INN-115: What would happen to customer choice if one partner controlled the interface?
- INN-116: Which measurement requires data a partner has no incentive to share?
- INN-117: What common definition is needed before partners can compare results?
- INN-118: Which disagreement concerns values rather than missing information?
- INN-119: What evidence would show that coordination itself has become the main cost?
- INN-120: Which promise should be narrowed until the missing complement is available?
